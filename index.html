Who We Are

A Blood Bank Born Inside a Hospital

NIGL Blood Centre is a licensed, hospital-based blood bank operating within Renova NIGL Hospital, Road No. 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad. Being embedded within a full-service hospital means every unit of blood we collect, screen, and store is immediately accessible to patients in critical need — with no delays.

🩺

We serve not just NIGL Hospital patients, but also support partner Renova hospitals, cancer care centres, and emergency cases across Hyderabad.

Every unit is tested for HIV, Hepatitis B & C, Malaria, Syphilis, and other transfusion-transmissible infections — ensuring the highest standards of blood safety for every recipient.

🏥 Blood Banking Services

  • 🔴

    Whole Blood Collection

    Voluntary and replacement donations accepted. Full screening and cross-matching performed on every unit.

  • 🧪

    Component Separation

    Red Blood Cells, Platelets, FFP, Cryoprecipitate, PRP, and Single Donor Platelets for targeted therapy.

  • 🛡️

    Infectious Disease Screening

    Every unit tested for HIV, HBsAg, HCV, Malaria, and Syphilis as per NBTC guidelines.

  • 🚑

    Emergency Blood Supply

    24 × 7 emergency issue for trauma, surgery, cancer care, and obstetric emergencies.

  • 📋

    Donor Registration & Records

    Lifetime donor records maintained. Voluntary donor pool for scheduled repeat donations.

Why It Matters

Why Donate Blood?

Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from generous donors like you. Here's what your single donation achieves.

🩺

One Donation, Three Lives

A single whole blood donation is separated into red cells, platelets, and plasma — each component saving a different patient in critical need.

🏥

Support Critical Care

Accident victims, cancer patients on chemotherapy, surgical patients, and mothers during childbirth all depend on a steady blood supply every single day.

💪

Good for Your Health

Regular donation reduces excess iron, stimulates new blood cell production, and includes a free health screening — as beneficial for donors as for recipients.
